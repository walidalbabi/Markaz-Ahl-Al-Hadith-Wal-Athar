ما الذي يُحذف

حسابك، وملفك الشخصي (الاسم الظاهر، والنبذة، والصورة، والجنس، والمرحلة والمستوى)، وطلب انضمامك، وكل رسائلك ورسائلك الصوتية وصورك وملفاتك، وتفاعلاتك، وإشعاراتك، وسجل حضورك للفصول، ورموز الإشعارات. تُحذف الملفات من التخزين تلقائياً بعد حذف الحساب.

ما الذي يبقى

تسجيلات الدروس المنشورة في مكتبة المركز تبقى؛ فإن كنت تحدثت على المنصة في درس مسجَّل فصوتك جزء من ذلك التسجيل. وتبقى الدروس التي بدأتها والمراجع التي رفعتها إن كنت معلّماً، بلا اسمك. ويبقى سجل إجراءات المشرفين بعد فصله عن اسمك. ولا يمكن حذف حساب آخر مشرف عام (مدير) قبل تعيين مدير غيره.

How to request deletion

In the app (fastest): Settings → "حذف الحساب" (Delete account) → type the word «حذف» to confirm. Your account is deleted immediately and you are signed out. If your membership application is still pending or was declined, the same option is on the application screen.

Without the app: if you no longer have the app, email the address below from the address linked to your account (the Google email you signed in with), with the subject "Account deletion request":

markazahlalhadithwalathar@gmail.com

We check that the request comes from the account holder, delete the account within 30 days at most, and confirm to you.

What is deleted

Your account, your profile (display name, bio, photo, gender, stage and level), your membership application, all your messages, voice notes, images and files, your reactions, notifications, class attendance records and notification tokens. Files are removed from storage automatically after the account is deleted.

What is kept

Lecture recordings already published in the center's library stay; if you spoke on stage in a recorded class, your voice is part of that recording. Classes you started and resources you uploaded (if you are a teacher) stay, without your name. The supervisors' action log stays, unlinked from your name. The last remaining administrator can't delete their account until another administrator is appointed.

Full details are in the privacy policy.
